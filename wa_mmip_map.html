Washington MMIP Jurisdiction Map

What this shows
This map combines Washington MMIP case records with the statewide jurisdiction framework so each case can be reviewed by year and by the entity that appears to hold legal authority.
Washington legal framework
Framework: PL280 Optional (1957-1963) - a genuine checkerboard: 8 specific subject-matter areas apply statewide, PLUS full state jurisdiction on non-Indian ('fee') land within reservations, PLUS full jurisdiction on any reservation whose tribe affirmatively requested it; a 2012 state law then enabled tribally-initiated retrocession, and several tribes (Yakama in 2014, and others) have since retroceded fully back to federal/tribal jurisdiction

Contradictory: YES - flagship example

Widely cited by legal scholars (e.g., Prof. Robert Anderson, Univ. of Washington) as the clearest illustration of PL280's incoherence: three independent variables (subject matter, land status, and tribe-specific retrocession) must each be checked before an officer even knows who has jurisdiction over a given incident.
